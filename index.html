    Our story

    More than a coloring book.

    Crayon Chic began with a newborn daughter and a simple wish: to build something small, beautiful and lasting for her. Founder Ray Safi comes from the world of technology, so he knows how much of childhood now happens on a screen. In the age of AI, he set out to bring back something wonderfully real. A page. A crayon. Time to slow down.

    The name

    Crayon is French for pencil. Chic means premium. Together, a simple promise: everyday coloring, made with uncommon care.

    For parents

    A gentle way to teach a culture, a cuisine, a language. Every book turns a subject into pages your child will want to finish.

    For grown-ups

    A quiet hour and a clear mind. More detailed designs for adults are part of where Crayon Chic is going.

    Ray grew up a perfectionist: detail-oriented, passionate, and always noticing the little things. That care goes into every page. One day we’d love to draw the same dish twice, simple for kids and detailed for grown-ups, so you can color side by side.
